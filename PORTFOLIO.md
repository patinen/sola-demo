# SOLA Studio — Beauty website concept

**Category:** Beauty / wellness.  
**Description:** A fictional slow-beauty studio concept designed as a warm, premium editorial experience. Its pacing, composition, and booking touchpoint are tailored to a calm personal-service brand.

**Design goals:** Create a soft, sophisticated identity and make treatments easy to discover.  
**Technologies:** Next.js, React, TypeScript, CSS.  
**Key features:** Responsive navigation, keyboard-dismissible accessible demo booking dialog, reduced-motion support, noindex metadata.  
**Project card:** Editorial beauty-studio concept with warm, atmospheric styling.  
**Suggested screenshots:** Hero desktop; treatment grid; booking section; mobile navigation.
